Explanation
Form 990 governing body review Part VI line 11
THE 990 AND ALL ACCOMPANYING DOCUMENTATION WILL BE REVIEWED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS FOR DISCUSSION AND APPROVAL. ONCE THE 990 IS APPROVED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, THE FORM 990 WILL BE SIGNED BY THE APPROPRIATE PARTIES AND FILED WITH THE IRS. COPIES OF THE COMPLETED 990 WILL BE MAINTAINED IN THE OFFICE OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE ASSISTANT.
Conflict of interest policy compliance Part VI line 12c
ALL MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS, WHETHER NOW SERVING OR LATER ELECTED, SHALL BE INFORMED OF THE BARTLETT CENTERS POLICIES WITH RESPECT TO CONFLICTS OF INTEREST. THE CHAIR SHALL BE NOTIFIED OF ANY CONFLICT OF INTEREST ARISING BETWEEN THE BARTLETT CENTER AND ANY MEMBER OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS. THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS SHALL BE NOTIFIED OF ANY CONFLICT OF INTEREST ARISING BETWEEN THE BARTLETT CENTER AND ANY MEMBER OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS IF SUCH CONFLICT OF INTEREST IS RELEVANT TO ANY MATTER REQUIRING ACTION BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS. ANY SUCH INTERESTED DIRECTOR SHALL DISCLOSE TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS THE NATURE OF THE CONFLICT AND SHALL PROVIDE THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS WITH ANY INFORMATION RELEVANT TO THE CONFLICTED MATTER. SUCH CONFLICTED DIRECTOR SHALL LEAVE THE MEETING, ETC.
Governing documents etc available to public Part VI line 19
THE ORGANIZATION MAKES ITS GOVERNING DOCUMENTS, CONFLICTS OF INTEREST POLICY AND FINANCIAL STATEMENTS AVAILABLE UPON REQUEST.
