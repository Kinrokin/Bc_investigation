## Rights and timestamps
Original sources retain their rights and credit. Download time is not event time. File hashes prove which bytes were preserved, not authenticity of every claim. No inference of unlawful conduct follows from missing, stale, or inaccessible sources. The complete master catalogue and dated research artifacts are in the separately distributed160-file collected edition.
