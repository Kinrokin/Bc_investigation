# Remaining records and source-first protocol

**Draft / not sent. No fees authorized.** These are acquisition questions, not allegations or compulsory demands on private parties.

## September contact and proposed operation
Seek City-held records identifying the announcement referred to in the September 28 release, when the City received it, first approaches and responses from United Way/YMCA, the provider referenced in the earlier board account, and the communication concerning Williams's transition assistance. Request actual agreements, drafts, approvals, budgets, funding commitments, effective dates and responsibility for staff, premises, insurance and first payroll.

## Alleged funding condition
Start with the person who reported it, voluntarily and without suggesting names: "Please describe the September 17 discussion in your own sequence. When you said 'we were told,' whom did 'we' include, who spoke the words you heard, and did you hear an outside statement firsthand or a retelling? What exact resource was discussed?"

Distinguish exact words from paraphrase, a funding decision from a board assessment, originator from messenger, firsthand attendees from people who heard later, and knowledge on September 17 from information learned September 18. Seek contemporaneous notes or original correspondence voluntarily. Obtain other accounts independently before disclosing another witness's answer. Explain intended use and attribution terms; do not promise absolute confidentiality or request privileged/private household material.

## Financial reconstruction
Obtain award-by-award recognition and receipt schedules for the combined governmental-grants category; claim submissions/attempts, deficiency notices, corrections, agency decisions, authorizations, cancellations, offsets and remittances; account/DVN/vendor crosswalks. Both known DVNs, 000550069 and historical 002688266, belong in scope without assuming two active capacities. Obtain actual payroll funding deadline and aggregate need, available unrestricted cash and the assistance requests and responses. The $15,000 bridge example was hypothetical, not a Bartlett figure.

## CDBG and City property
Obtain original 2024–2025 contract drafts and identified correspondence, final governance clauses, actual appointments/powers, matched comparator agreements, claim-specific holds and release records, applicable award conditions and actual HUD draw availability. Obtain the current write-off policy and receivable ledger. Distinguish accounting write-off, collection pause and legal release. Match the particular operating/property transaction to the relevant lease and approval route; do not assume emergency procurement.

## Corporate and meal-program records
Obtain completed SOS filings, AG notice/receipt/plan or summary and supplements, asset ownership and actual conveyances. Blank Corp.45 is not a filed Bartlett form. Ask public custodians for retained board rosters, governing documents and actual action records. Ask DHSS for actual CACFP participation/site agreements and institutional access-change approvals with sensitive personal data redacted; blank official questionnaires and access forms are not completed Bartlett records.

## Long-term use
Obtain any creation instrument for the stakeholder group, mandate, members, reporting line, records custodian, notices, minutes and RFP criteria. Ask whether childcare continuity is mandatory or one possible use, which rooms are involved, and what deed/capital-funding restrictions govern the site. Multiple future tenants do not alone establish elimination of childcare or a decided repurposing.

## Records language
Seek existing nonexempt records with segregable portions and withholding reasons, available native attachments/metadata and source versions; not newly created legal opinions. Qualifying public-business records may be on personal systems, but this is not a demand for whole-device access. Ask for a cost estimate before charges. A public-custodian route and a voluntary private-party request are different.
