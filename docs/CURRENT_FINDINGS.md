# Bartlett / Horace Mann — connected findings

**Collected-record cutoff: September 28, 2026, before the evening Council meeting.** Source preservation was improved later that afternoon. This is an AI-assisted research synthesis, not a finding of legal liability or political culpability.

## 1. What is established
The collected record supports a financial crisis predating September, a disputed executive separation during proposed provider-transition discussions, and a public decision to explore an interim childcare arrangement. The City had property and CDBG-administration roles, Bartlett's board controlled corporate decisions, and state agencies administered separate licensing, subsidy and meal-program processes. Those roles create identifiable records to inspect; they do not prove a common motive or instruction.

The September 28 City/United Way/YMCA release identifies YMCA as developing a **tentative 120-day childcare plan**. It does not identify an executed agreement or a permanent selection. Its relative sequence places City outreach after an announcement of intended dissolution and before the board's approval, without supplying the first-contact date. It does not establish outreach before the disputed September 18 employment event.

The September 21 release described a future permanent childcare provider. The September 28 release describes possible future uses and one or more tenants. That changes the public scope of planning, not proof of an adopted repurposing decision. The lease did not grant Bartlett the entire building or exclusive childcare use: Rooms 8–11 were excluded and the program description included broader family/youth/community services.

## 3. Audit and liquidity
The February 18, 2026 audit gives an unmodified financial-statement opinion while identifying substantial doubt about continuing operations. Management's going-concern explanation discusses public-payment delays, funding reductions and longer philanthropic cycles. It is management's explanation in the statements, not an adjudication of every agency's conduct.

The Government Auditing Standards report identifies significant deficiency2025-1: insufficient segregation of financial duties. It reports no identified material weakness and no reportable noncompliance from its limited tests. A control weakness is not evidence of theft, and an audit opinion is not a guarantee of absence of misconduct. The $1,000 purchase approval in management's response is Bartlett's internal control, not the City's debt-write-off ceiling.

The eight-month February2026 management accounts show $380,842.56 revenue, $426,463.84 expenses and a $45,621.28 deficit. They do not reveal September unrestricted cash, the next payroll amount or a bank covenant. The later public accounts discuss inability to meet payroll, but a named bank-trigger event and the actual bridge request remain unverified.

## 4. CDBG: reconstruct the request, not just the service month
The City-prepared chronology records delayed contract execution, missing documents and report-format issues, then payments. It is not each underlying email or a bank-settlement history. The August16,2024 draft to March26,2025 signature interval is222days, not nine months after the draft. It falls in the ninth month of a July-start fiscal year, a different measure.

The November11 entry references board-minutes and City-representative provisions. The actual wording, powers, final acceptance and appointment are missing. An observer, liaison, voting director and contractual approval right are different mechanisms.

The May–June2025 reimbursement group is recorded received August29 and paid September12:14days. The July–September2025 group is recorded received October21 and paid December12:52days, including49days before referral to Finance. The latter table references missing income statements and a government-shutdown notation; combined-report issues elsewhere should not be automatically assigned to this interval.

The 30-calendar-day reimbursement provision has an exception for requests reasonably believed improper. Receipt, correction, approval, agency draw, internal Finance referral and settlement must be distinguished. The October16,2025 HUD lapse plan said previously obligated CDBG money remained available for drawdown where further review was unnecessary. That defeats a universal shutdown explanation, not the possibility of a particular funding restriction.

## 6. Personnel: the unresolved source and meaning
Williams publicly described being told that help/funding would not come while she remained, in an account of the September17 discussion. The board describes an offered resignation followed by acceptance; she disputes that. The City acknowledges declining transition assistance while denying requesting resignation or exchanging debt relief for removal. The earlier board account attributes lack of need for her help to an unnamed provider.

The decisive questions are who originated the funding statement, whether it was heard firsthand or relayed, which resource was meant, what authority the speaker had and what decision it influenced. The September17 funding statement and September18 transition-assistance decision are not proven to be the same message. Today's YMCA identification does not independently name the earlier provider or the alleged original speaker.

A resource could be one bridge payment rather than the whole annual budget. But a$15,000 shortfall, a bank call, a donor ultimatum, board faction or intent to manufacture errors cannot be supplied without evidence. Source-first accounts and actual requests/offers matter more than more annual totals.

## 7. Corporate, premises and licensing acts must stay separate
The September25 resolution authorizes dissolution steps; it is not a filed Corp.45, AG receipt or deed. Effective articles, permitted winding-up activity, applicable AG notice and actual asset conveyance are different events. The public plan's generic residual-beneficiary language justifies obtaining the full submitted plan or summary and supplements, not automatically declaring the process void.

Missouri's default written-consent rule does not automatically validate or void the reported text action without the actual governing documents and action record. A defect in one employment act would not automatically invalidate all later board decisions. Current membership rights, director roster and powers remain necessary.

The lease has distinct mechanisms for access, rentals, assignment, amendments and termination. Fixture retention is conditional, not a universal conversion of Bartlett property into City property. A certificate of insurance does not amend the policy. Relevant coverage, insured activity and actual party roles must be examined.

No120-day cap appears in the retrieved City emergency-procurement provision. Its application depends on the transaction; an occupancy arrangement is not automatically a public-fund purchase. The actual proposed start trigger is also missing.

The primary retrieved DVN000550069 covers birth through12/capacity94. Historical DVN002688266 covered school-age care/capacity40. Those capacities cannot be added without establishing concurrent authorization; a blank historical summary does not establish program elimination. A child authorization, facility license, provider subsidy contract, vendor registration and meal-sponsor agreement are different records.
