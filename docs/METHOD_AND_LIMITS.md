# Method, privacy and evidentiary limits

## Source hierarchy
An original public record is different from a page extract, an agency's retrospective chronology, an attributed interview, an AI-assisted summary or an unverified browser transcription. A repeated press statement is not a second witness. A hash authenticates the file version preserved, not the factual truth or origin of every assertion.

## Four separate questions
1. What caused financial deterioration and liquidity pressure?
2. What authority governed the actual corporate, premises and operating acts?
3. Was a particular resource made conditional on a personnel outcome, by whom and with what effect?
4. Who commissioned the political message and survey, and was that party connected to the employment decision?

These questions do not stand or fall together. A lawful contract could coexist with an improper condition; a paperwork problem could exist without coordinated retaliation.

## No invented acts
Requests are drafts, not submissions. Interview questions are not conducted interviews. An agenda item is not a vote; a vote to negotiate is not a signed contract; a release is not a regulatory approval; a certificate is not a policy; a future term is not a verified start date. The research cutoff predates the September 28 evening meeting.

## Political messages
Recovered crops in the complete collected edition show electoral framing, not an authenticated payer, sender, calendar timestamp, complete questionnaire or proof of their assertions. VoterCX/Qualtrics infrastructure, candidate committees, intermediaries and ultimate payer remain different layers. A separate Wyoming lead is not evidence of Missouri sponsorship. Publicly repeated allegations remain attributed allegations.

## Privacy and public sharing
The public edition excludes raw private working-chat/profile/workflow material and sensitive unrelated contacts. No children's or household subsidy records, private bank credentials, or privileged legal communications are requested or represented. Government sources retain their original official/business contact details where appropriate. A separate private archive retains historical inputs unchanged.

## Versioning and correction
Retain dates, source identities, original page numbers and known limitations. Do not erase a prior report when correcting it. Label what a new record changes and what it does not. Neither source count nor narrative confidence substitutes for the missing firsthand link.
