# Portfolio case study: auditable public-records synthesis

## Project
Robert King organized an AI-assisted investigation of a Missouri nonprofit childcare transition around source provenance, financial reconciliation, legal-authority questions and testable gaps. The result is an evidence collection rather than a verdict about a political actor.

## Work demonstrated
- Reconstructed the fiscal-year contribution bridge using exact classifications rather than equating revenue, state payments and cash.
- Separated a $512,652 contribution-category decline into its components while leaving the $321,135 governmental-grant component unallocated by award.
- Distinguished the $263,980 accounting deficit from the $17,170 cash decrease, and the $98,770 lease-liability recognition from operating arrears.
- Used Decimal arithmetic to test a candidate four-cent rent carryover and printed schedule inconsistencies without claiming the City's ledger was thereby proved.
- Preserved distinct decision clocks and legal roles for the City, nonprofit board, regulator, prospective operator, employer and meal sponsor.
- Corrected initial theories when counterevidence appeared: United Way assessment payment, donated land, lease scope, temporary term, historical DVN and the limits of message metadata.
- Organized reusable tables, catalogs, workbooks, source-document copies, witness questions, draft requests and validation scripts.

## Deliverables
The separately distributed 160-file collected edition includes the 44-page casebook, political-message file, 16-page adversarial review, September 28 amendment, 17-sheet fiscal/evidence workbook and 6-sheet reconciliation workbook. Its original master dossier contains 62 chronology entries, 30 actor/capacity entries, 36 tested propositions and a 103-entry source/artifact register. These are inventory counts, not independent confirmations or measures of guilt.

This Git edition publishes a readable case study, source documents, core tables and exact-arithmetic checks. The emailed collected ZIP remains the complete report-preservation edition; this branch is not represented as its byte-identical mirror.

## Transferable method
Source identification; raw-versus-derived labeling; exact arithmetic; uncertainty attached to the relevant proposition; correction without erasing history; privacy-separated publication; and a boundary between analysis, draft action and executed action.

## Limits
The collection does not authenticate the alleged funding-for-departure condition, identify the text/survey commissioner, certify an executed operator transition or supply a judicial finding. A large archive does not compensate for the missing original communication or transaction.
